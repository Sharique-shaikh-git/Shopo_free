## 4. FAQ Document

### General Questions

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Digital Dukaan kya hai?** | Digital Dukaan ek mobile app hai jisse aap apni dukaan online bana sakte hain. |
| **Kitna kharcha hai?** | Free plan hai. Paid plan 500 PKR/month se shuru hota hai. |
| **Technical knowledge chahiye?** | Nahi! Sirf phone chalana aana chahiye. AI sab kuch karega. |
| **Payment kaise milta hai?** | Cash on Delivery, JazzCash, EasyPaisa se payment mil sakta hai. |

### Product Management

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Product kaise add karein?** | Camera se photo lein, AI automatically details generate kar dega. |
| **Price kaise rakhein?** | AI suggested price dega. Aap change kar sakte hain. |
| **Stock kaise track karein?** | Har product mein stock add karein. App automatically track karega. |

### Order Management

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Order kaise aata hai?** | Notification aayega. App mein jaake accept karein. |
| **Customer ko kaise batayein?** | WhatsApp se automatically message jayega. |
| **Delivery kaise karein?** | Apni delivery安排 karein ya partner service use karein. |

### Payments

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Payment kaise setup karein?** | Settings > Payment Methods mein JazzCash/EasyPaisa add karein. |
| **Kitna charges hai?** | 2% transaction fee hai. |
| **Paise kab milte hain?** | Weekly settlement hota hai. |
