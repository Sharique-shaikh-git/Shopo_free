# AI Shop Builder (Pakistan-first) — Product & Project Idea Document (PID)

## 1) Vision (What we’re building)
Build a **mobile-first, AI-powered online shop system** for **non-technical merchants in Pakistan** (shopkeepers, home businesses, Instagram/WhatsApp sellers, housewives, freelancers). 

The goal: **“Open the app → take photos / answer simple questions → start receiving orders (COD-first).”**

We are not selling “website building.” We are enabling a merchant to **run an online store like a shop**—product pages replace back-and-forth chats, so customers see price/details in one place and merchants get orders faster.

---

## 2) Core Problem (Why it exists)
### 2.1 WhatsApp/Facebook selling pain
- Too much chat: price negotiation, feature questions, “original?” “size?” “warranty?” etc.
- Leads take time; many customers leave after long bargaining.
- Merchants spend energy replying instead of selling.

### 2.2 Current e-commerce platforms pain
- Shopify-like platforms are perceived as **expensive**.
- Technical terms (domain, hosting, SSL, templates, SEO menus, etc.) overwhelm non-technical users.
- Setup and management often assume desktop usage.

### 2.3 Market reality
- Most small merchants use **mobile**.
- Most trust **Cash on Delivery (COD)**.
- Most don’t know technical terms; they need **conversational guidance + automation**.

---

## 3) Target Users (Who it’s for)
Start with “Everyone like the same cycle” but choose a first launch segment:
- Clothing sellers
- Cosmetics & beauty sellers
- Grocery/home essentials sellers
- Mobile accessories sellers
- Jewelry & accessories sellers
- Home businesses/housewives selling products
- Instagram/WhatsApp resellers
- Small freelancers selling services/products digitally (later)

Geography (Phase 1): **Pakistan**. 
Expansion: Bangladesh, Nepal, India, Indonesia, Middle East, USA.

---

## 4) Product Positioning (How we describe it)
**Do not lead with “AI website builder.”**

Position as:
- **“Create your online shop in minutes from your phone.”**
- **“Get orders without technical work.”**
- **“Your shop + products + customer-facing ordering—automatic.”**

---

## 5) Key Outcomes (What success looks like)
1. Merchant creates a store in **10–15 minutes** with almost no typing.
2. Customer can browse products on mobile and place orders immediately.
3. Merchant receives orders with clear details and can manage fulfillment from mobile.
4. Promotion and discovery help reduce merchant effort (ads + share).
5. Merchants see business value quickly: orders + repeat customers.

---

## 6) User Experience (UX) — Simple onboarding + daily flow
### 6.1 Merchant onboarding flow
1. **Choose language**: Urdu / Sindhi / Balochi / (others later) / English.
2. **Business type** (optional conversational selection): “What do you sell?”
3. **Shop name** (simple input or voice)
4. **Logo** (upload or AI generates a basic logo)
5. **Upload first product**: photo
6. **AI auto-completes**:
   - Product title
   - Description
   - Category
   - Brand/attributes (when possible)
   - Tags/keywords
   - Suggested selling price range
7. Merchant confirms: **“Use this?”**
8. Store gets a **free subdomain** immediately (COD-ready).

### 6.2 Daily merchant home (mobile-first)
- **New Orders** (tap to view)
- **Ship / Mark fulfilled**
- **Stock alerts** (low stock)
- **Promote my shop** (one-tap ad creation / sharing)
- **Today/This week sales** (simple counters)

---

## 7) Customer Experience (Buyer flow)
1. Customer clicks a link from ad/post
2. Mobile store loads fast
3. Product page shows:
   - clear image(s)
   - title + description in local language
   - price
   - delivery info (COD)
   - seller/store info (trust)
4. Customer places order with:
   - name, phone
   - delivery address
   - optional notes
5. Merchant gets order notification.

---

## 8) AI Features (Automation that matters)
### 8.1 Product creation from photo
- OCR/vision to infer product name/brand
- AI text generator for description
- Attribute inference (depends on category)
- Multi-language generation for store content

### 8.2 Price suggestion (important differentiator)
- Provide **suggested price** or **price range** based on:
  - similar product listings
  - market references (curated sources)
- UI must clearly label: **“Suggested price”**
- Allow merchant to override.

### 8.3 Store front generation
- Auto-build:
  - homepage layout
  - product grid
  - categories
  - about/contact pages (minimal)
  - theme color palette based on merchant choice
- Keep design simple (premium on mobile is not required; readability + trust matter).

### 8.4 Voice input + voice translation
- Voice-to-text for shop/product entry
- AI generates content in chosen languages

---

## 9) Store URLs / Domains
### 9.1 Free subdomain (like Shopify)
Every store gets:
- `shopname.appdomain.pk` (or similar)

No domain purchase required to start.

### 9.2 Custom domain (later / upsell)
- Merchant can purchase/attach domain
- Domain setup should be one-click
- Backend handles DNS details automatically.

---

## 10) Payments & Logistics
### 10.1 Payments (Phase 1)
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)** as default (most users)
- Optional local payments (wallet/bank transfer) later

### 10.2 Logistics (Phase 1)
- Merchant receives order → chooses/requests courier pickup
- System supports:
  - courier pickup booking
  - shipping label generation
  - delivery tracking (where available)

### 10.3 Order status flow
- New → Packed → Shipped → Delivered (and optional returns/cancel rules)

---

## 11) Marketing & Growth Tools (Must reduce merchant effort)
Merchants struggle to run ads and explain products.

### 11.1 One-tap promotions
- “Boost product” / “Promote my shop”
- Create:
  - Facebook/Instagram ad copy + creatives suggestions
  - WhatsApp promo message templates
  - Short product captions for social posts

### 11.2 WhatsApp sharing flow
- One-tap share product link
- Auto-generated message in local language

### 11.3 Ads integration concept
- UI guides merchant step-by-step in plain language
- If full ad integration isn’t possible early, we provide:
  - content + instructions + copy/paste prompts
  - campaign goals explanation without technical terms

---

- Set/update stock (where supported)
- View orders and fulfillment statuses
- Low-stock notifications
- Basic analytics:
  - today sales, this month sales
  - total orders
  - best-selling product

Keep analytics **simple**.

   - free subdomain

## 15) MVP Definition (What to build first)
### MVP goals
- Merchant can create a store with 1–3 products and get a live ordering page.
- Customer can browse and place a COD order.
- Merchant can view and fulfill orders.

### MVP scope (minimum set)
**Merchant app (mobile-first)**
- onboarding (language → shop name → product photo upload)
- AI product auto-fill + confirm
- store link generation (subdomain)
- product management (add/edit)
- orders dashboard (new orders + mark fulfilled)
- stock update (optional but recommended)
- share product link / store link

**Customer storefront (mobile web)**
- home + categories + product pages
- order form (COD)

**Backend services**
- auth + store management
- product catalog storage
- order capture
- notifications (email/SMS/WhatsApp optional)
- AI generation endpoints (product text + translation + title/description)

---

## 16) Non-Functional Requirements (quality)
- Performance: fast mobile page load
- Accessibility: readable typography and large buttons
- Reliability: order capture must be accurate
- Localization: RTL support where needed (Urdu)
- Security: protect merchant data, prevent abuse

---

## 17) Competitive Strategy (How we win)
- Not “AI features.” Win by **removing complexity**.
- Win by **mobile-first** for merchants.
- Win by **COD-first** and local market fit.
- Win by **chat-to-commerce conversion**: website answers questions immediately.
- Win by **price suggestion**.

---

## 18) Project Plan (Phases) — from idea to production
### Phase 0: Discovery (1–2 weeks)
- interview 20–30 merchants (shopkeepers, home sellers)
- identify:
  - top 30 product categories
  - most asked customer questions
  - acceptable pricing UI and price suggestion trust
- validate COD flow and courier availability

### Phase 1: Design + Prototype (2–4 weeks)
- create clickable prototype
- test onboarding speed and comprehension (non-technical users)
- run A/B tests for:
  - onboarding copy
  - voice vs typing adoption

### Phase 2: MVP Build (6–10 weeks)
- merchant mobile app
- customer storefront
- order system
- AI product generation v1
- notifications + fulfillment actions

### Phase 3: Launch (2–4 weeks)
- pilot with 50–200 merchants
- measure:
  - store creation completion rate
  - orders per store per week
  - content acceptance rate (AI suggestions)

### Phase 4: Growth Features
- marketing automation and promotion tools
- improved AI extraction for category-specific attributes
- custom domain onboarding
- payment expansion beyond COD

---

## 19) Metrics (KPIs)
- Activation rate: % merchants who publish first store
- AI acceptance: % products where merchant accepts AI-generated fields
- Time-to-first-order
- Orders per merchant per week
- Store page conversion: product view → order
- Support ticket volume (should drop vs WhatsApp-only)

---

## 20) Risks & Mitigations
- **AI accuracy risk**: start with conservative AI; allow quick corrections
- **Price suggestion trust**: label as suggestion, provide sources/consistency
- **Courier coverage**: start with top cities
- **Fraud risk (COD)**: verification steps + merchant reputation
- **Ad integration complexity**: begin with content + guided steps

---

## 21) Definition of Done (for MVP)
- A merchant can:
  1) create store
  2) upload at least 1 product via photo
  3) publish store
  4) receive COD orders
  5) mark order fulfilled

- A customer can:
  1) visit store
  2) view product
  3) place COD order

---

## 22) One-liner summary (PID elevator)
**A mobile-first AI “shop assistant” for Pakistani non-technical merchants: auto-generate products, answer customer questions instantly, provide COD-ready ordering, and help merchants promote—all without technical website knowledge.**
